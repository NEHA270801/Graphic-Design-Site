ABOUT THE CLUB

MADE BY STUDENT, FOR STUDENTS

The Graphic Design Club is a student-run community at ST. CLAIR COLLEGE. We make things together, show each other the work and publish what we learn.

HOUSE RULE

CRITIQUE THE
WORK, NOT
THE DESIGNER.

WHO WE ARE

A community of Graphic Design students who like making things and like making them better.

WHAT WE DO

Run workshops, design challenges, portfolio reviews and events, then write up what we learned.

WHO IT'S FOR

Graphic Design students first. Other creative programs, prospective students are welcome.

WHY JOIN

Work improves when other people see it. You get feedback, practice, portfolio pieces and people to make things with.

DESIGN STORY

WHERE AI STILL FAILS

AI fails in graphic design because it lacks strategic problem-solving, emotional nuance, and original vision. It cannot build complex design systems, producing generic, formulaic templates that lack human authenticity.

WHAT THE CLUB DOES

CLUB ACTIVITIES

WORKSHOPS

Hands- on sessions in the tools and techniques we all end up needing.

HOW TO TAKE PART

Bring your laptop , i-pad and questions. No experience needed.

DESIGN CHALLENGES

One prompt, a deadline and a room full of slam.

HOW TO TAKE PART

Pick up the prompt, submit by the deadline.

PORTFOLIO REVIEWS

Show your work to people who will tell you the truth kindly.

HOW TO TAKE PART

Book a slot and leave with a to-do list.

GUEST SPEAKERS

Designers, alumni and studio people on how the work really happens.

HOW TO TAKE PART

Send a question in advance.

CREATIVE EVENTS

Poster jams, screenings, field trips and show and tell nights.

HOW TO TAKE PART

Watch the events calendar below.

PEER LEARNING

HOW TO TAKE PART
